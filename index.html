Non-negotiables in every ad

Rules that keep it big and honest

Bundle shows big

The Master Box plus cartridges fill at least 40% of the frame in every ad, including memes and phone screens. You can tell what is for sale in under a second.

Name the buyer

A qualifier line above every hook, rotating: D2C founders, performance marketers, ecommerce agencies, Shopify and Woo brands, media buyers, solo founders.

Price in every frame

₹699 once, always, with a value anchor beside it. CTA verb is unique to each ad, never repeated inside a style.

Add-ons stay add-ons

The ₹699 bundle is 16 agents + 391 files + 3 bonuses. The 4 add-ons and the free swipe-file gift never appear as included.

No fake proof

No invented ROAS, revenue, star ratings or customer quotes. Chats and memes are clearly dramatised; no real faces, no protected characters, no real brands beyond Claude and ChatGPT.

Built for the feed

Hook 2 to 7 words, readable at phone size, 6% safe border, one device per ad, no two neighbouring ads share a palette.
